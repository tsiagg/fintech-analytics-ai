01 — Positioning

An analytics engineering portfolio

An end-to-end analytics stack for a simulated retail trading broker. The project turns trades, deposits, withdrawals, signups, acquisition costs and regional targets into trusted decision support.

dbt builds the warehouse and a semantic layer of 57 governed metrics. Airflow runs the daily pipeline. Streamlit exposes the results through an executive dashboard, a chat BI assistant and an automatically generated CFO report.

Most “AI on top of your data” demos let a language model write SQL against raw tables. That produces confident, plausible, wrong answers. This project takes the opposite approach: net revenue is defined once, in dbt and MetricFlow, with tests around it. When the LLM produces a sentence, every number in it came out of the warehouse.

The warehouse, models, metrics, tests, orchestration and AI guardrails are real and running. The source data is synthetic — deliberately, because an analytics engineer would not generate source data. The simulator stands in for the upstream product.
