## Summary

I work where data analysis meets analytics engineering: the analyst who answers the commercial question, and the engineer who makes the number trustworthy enough to reuse.

Three years in the Business Intelligence team of a retail broker have been spent querying trading-platform data, modelling business logic in SQL and dbt, and putting the result in front of decision-makers through Tableau, Power BI and structured reporting. The through-line is the same in a dashboard and in a warehouse: define the metric once, test it, and let every surface — including AI — read from that definition.

I am drawn to relational structure, statistics and pattern recognition, but I treat those as tools for a practical outcome: automate the path from raw activity to a figure a stakeholder can act on, and can defend.
